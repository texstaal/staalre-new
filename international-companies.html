Frequently asked

Setting up a warehouse in the Netherlands from abroad

Can we lease a warehouse in the Netherlands without visiting?

Yes. The search, viewings, checks and negotiation are done here, and you see every building on video and in a written report. Leases can usually be signed electronically, and a purchase can be completed at the notary with a power of attorney. We recommend one visit before you sign a long lease, but it is your call.

Do we need a Dutch company to lease a warehouse?

In practice, yes. Landlords want a Dutch BV or a registered branch with a KvK number, a Dutch business bank account and security such as a parent-company guarantee or a bank guarantee. A notary can set up a BV online, and we run the warehouse search at the same time. See how to get set up to lease.

How long does it take to find a warehouse from abroad?

For an existing building, plan on roughly three to six months from the first call to the keys. It depends on what is available at your size and on how quickly your Dutch entity, bank account and guarantee are ready, so start the paperwork early.

Should we use a 3PL or our own warehouse?

A 3PL suits small or uncertain volumes. Your own warehouse starts to pay off when volumes are steady, when you need assembly, repairs, spare parts or a showroom, or when you want your own team in Europe. Many companies run both for a while. See finding a warehouse from abroad.

Which region should we choose?

It depends on how your goods arrive and where your customers are. Rotterdam suits sea freight, Schiphol air freight, Venlo and Limburg the German market, and Tilburg–Waalwijk e-commerce. Labour and grid capacity differ by region too. See choosing a Dutch hub.

Can you work with our customs broker, 3PL or lawyer?

Yes. We work alongside the advisers you already have, and introduce a notary, lawyer, tax adviser or customs specialist where you need one. If you store non-EU goods under customs supervision, tell us at the start, because the building must meet customs requirements.

What does your service cost?

For a lease: no lease, no fee. You only pay if you sign for a space we found or negotiated for you, and part of the fee depends on what we negotiate off the landlord’s first offer. For a purchase we agree the fee with you in writing before we start.
