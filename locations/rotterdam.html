Warehouse Space in Rotterdam

Europe’s largest seaport and the Netherlands’ biggest distribution gateway.

Rotterdam is the Netherlands’ largest logistics gateway and home to Europe’s biggest seaport, which handled 435.8 million tonnes of cargo in 2024. For occupiers, warehousing here means direct access to deep-sea container flows, port drayage and the A15 corridor — at the highest rents and tightest land availability in the country.

01What Rotterdam is known for

The character of the market shapes what you should look for on the ground:

  • Deep-sea container and bulk flows through Maasvlakte, Botlek and Waalhaven
  • Port-edge distribution and drayage on the A15 corridor toward Ridderkerk and Barendrecht
  • Distripark Maasvlakte, deep-sea, shortsea and RoRo terminals, and rail/barge “extended-gate” links inland
  • Some of the largest — and scarcest — modern warehouse footprints in the country

Frequently asked

Warehousing in Rotterdam

Where do warehouses cluster around Rotterdam?

The heaviest concentration sits on the port itself — Maasvlakte, Botlek and Waalhaven — and along the A15 corridor east of the port toward Ridderkerk, Barendrecht and Dordrecht, where larger modern units and better land availability are found.

Why are Rotterdam warehouse rents higher than the rest of the Netherlands?

Land next to the terminals is scarce and demand from port-related occupiers is constant. That scarcity keeps prime Rotterdam logistics rents among the highest in the country, so structuring the lease well matters more here than almost anywhere else.
