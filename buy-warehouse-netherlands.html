Buy a Warehouse in the Netherlands, Buyer-Side Only

For companies that want to own the warehouse they operate from, from 500 m² to XXL, nationwide. We act for the buyer, never the seller.

Owning makes sense when your operation is stable, your fit-out is specialised, or the location is too scarce to rent. We find the building, test it, and negotiate the price and the purchase terms on your side, with one point of contact from brief to notary.

Specialised fit-out
Automation, cold storage or heavy racking that pays back over 10–15 years, longer than a sensible lease
Scarce locations
Port, border and urban sites where a lease renews on the landlord’s terms because there is no alternative
A long horizon
Proven volumes and a site you will use for a decade or more, where owning can beat total occupancy cost
Control
Your own solar, batteries, grid capacity and expansion plans, without landlord consent for every change
How buying works
01

Brief & ownership case

We define the building you need and model buying against leasing on total cost, so you know owning is the right call before you search.

02

Search, on and off market

Owner-occupier buildings are often sold quietly by private owners. We search listed stock and approach owners directly.

03

Assess

Location, building, zoning, grid capacity and price, tested against comparable sales before you make an offer.

04

Negotiate

Price, timing and the conditions in the purchase agreement: financing, due diligence and permits, so you can walk away if you need to.

We coordinate the technical, environmental, legal and tax checks with your advisers, or ours, and flag what should move the price.

06

Complete

Through the notary to transfer and registration in the Kadaster, then handover and, where needed, fit-out.

Frequently asked

Buying a warehouse in the Netherlands

Can a foreign company buy a warehouse in the Netherlands?

Yes. There are no restrictions on foreign ownership of Dutch commercial property. You can buy through your existing company or through a Dutch BV; the choice is mainly a tax and financing question, so settle it with your tax adviser before you sign. Our guide to buying a warehouse in the Netherlands walks through the full process.

What taxes do you pay when buying a warehouse in the Netherlands?

On an existing building, usually 10.4% transfer tax (overdrachtsbelasting) on the purchase price, paid by the buyer. A new building sold within two years of first use carries 21% VAT instead, and transfer tax then normally does not apply. See the full cost breakdown.

How long does it take to buy a warehouse in the Netherlands?

Once you have found the building, going from agreed terms to transfer at the notary commonly takes two to four months, depending on due diligence, financing and any permit conditions. How long the search takes depends on how scarce your type of building is in your preferred region.

Why are warehouses for sale hard to find?

Most large, modern warehouses are owned by institutional investors, who sell to other investors, usually with a tenant in place, not to occupiers. Buildings that suit an owner-occupier, especially between 500 and 5,000 m², are more often held by private owners and business owners, and many are sold without ever being openly listed. That is why we search off the market as well as on it.

Do you also act for sellers?

No. We act for the buyer or the tenant, never the seller, and we do not list property. That is why we can tell you plainly when a building is overpriced, or when leasing is the better option.

What is erfpacht and how does it affect a warehouse purchase?

Erfpacht is a Dutch ground lease: you own the building, but the land stays with the landowner, often a municipality or, in the port of Rotterdam, the port authority. You pay ground rent and must follow the lease conditions, which affect cost, use and resale. Check the conditions and the next rent revision before you sign.

Should I buy or lease a warehouse?

Lease while the operation is still proving itself or the location is uncertain; buy when your fit-out is specialised, the site is scarce, or your horizon is long and stable. Read lease or buy for a distribution operation, or see leasing warehouse space in the Netherlands.

Can you help with financing a warehouse purchase?

Yes. We introduce you to lenders and finance partners and give the bank the property information it needs. Expect a larger equity contribution than on a home, and close scrutiny of the operating company’s accounts. See our partners.
