What a European Warehouse in the Netherlands Costs in Year One (2026 Budget)

By Tex Staal · Founder & Advisor, Staal Real Estate
TL;DR
  • A 3,000 m² warehouse in one of the more affordable Dutch regions costs about €225,000 a year in rent and service charges.
  • With racking, two reach trucks, six operatives and a small showroom, year one comes to roughly €720,000–990,000 excluding VAT, energy, insurance and IT; €210,000–450,000 of that is one-off.
  • Plan for a bank guarantee of about three months of rent and service charges plus VAT (about €68,000 here).
  • Rent-free months, a fit-out contribution and an indexation cap are negotiable. See how we help companies from abroad.

What does a European warehouse in the Netherlands cost in its first year? Rent is only part of it. In year one you also pay for racking, forklifts, staff, fit-out and setup, and the landlord wants a guarantee before you get the keys. This guide builds a realistic first-year budget for a 3,000 m² warehouse run by a company from abroad, line by line, so headquarters can approve a number that holds.

The example

A company from outside the EU leases 3,000 m² (about 2,600 m² of hall and 400 m² of office and showroom) in one of the more affordable logistics regions, such as Venlo or Tilburg–Waalwijk, for five years. It stores about 2,000 pallets, runs the warehouse with six operatives and two reach trucks, and fits out a small showroom. All amounts exclude VAT.

About €210,000–450,000 of that is one-off. From year two, rent, service charges, forklifts and staff come to roughly €510,000–550,000 a year, before indexation, energy and insurance.

Rent and service charges

Rent depends mostly on the region. For modern space, prime rents run from about €55–72 per m² a year around Venlo to €80–100 around Rotterdam and €95–125 near Schiphol; the example uses €65, typical for the more affordable regions. On top come service charges for security, sprinkler maintenance, landscaping and shared areas, which we budget at about €5–15 per m² a year. See rent prices by region and what it costs to rent a warehouse.

Two things change the real number. Rent-free months: each one is worth about €16,000 in this example, and they are negotiable. Indexation: rent usually rises with inflation every year, so a cap is worth negotiating; see negotiating a warehouse lease.

The guarantee: cash you don't spend but can't use

Dutch landlords usually ask for a bank guarantee or deposit of about three months' rent plus service charges and VAT. Here that is about €68,000. A company from abroad without Dutch accounts may be asked for more, or for a parent-company guarantee. It is returned at the end of the lease, but plan for it in year one.

Racking and equipment

Standard pallet racking costs roughly €80–150 per pallet location installed, so 2,000 locations is about €160,000–300,000, depending on height, load and safety features. Racking above a certain height needs to fit the building's fire-safety rules, so check that before you order. For forklifts, Dutch lessors list electric reach trucks at about €1,100–1,550 a month on full-service lease; used or lighter trucks can be cheaper. Leasing keeps the first-year cash out low.

Staff

The Dutch minimum wage is €14.99 an hour for workers aged 21 and over from July 2026, and warehouse operatives typically earn about €15–18 an hour, more for reach-truck drivers. On top of gross pay come 8% holiday allowance (required by law), pension and employer social charges. As a planning figure we use about €43,000–47,000 a year per full-time operative, all-in; check it with a payroll provider for your sector agreement. Six operatives is then about €260,000–280,000, before a site manager. Many companies start with agency staff and hire permanently once volumes are stable.

Fit-out

Most existing Dutch units come with a basic office. A showroom, training room, workshop or extra offices for a European team usually needs work. Costs vary too much to give one figure: from tens of thousands for a refresh to well over €100,000 for a full showroom and workshop. Agree before you sign who pays for what, and what you must remove when you leave; landlords often contribute to fit-out on a longer lease.

Setup costs for a company from abroad

  • Dutch BV or branch: notary fee plus the Chamber of Commerce (KvK) registration fee, about €85 in 2026. Ask the notary for a fixed quote.
  • Business bank account: start early; it often takes longest.
  • VAT and import: a VAT number, and for goods from outside the EU an Article 23 licence for import VAT deferral, through a fiscal representative if you have no Dutch entity.
  • Advisers: a tax adviser and a lawyer to review the lease. See how to get set up to lease.

Costs to get quotes for

Energy, building and stock insurance, a warehouse management system, security and cleaning depend heavily on how you operate: charging electric trucks or testing machines uses far more power than plain storage. Ask the landlord for the building's energy use and grid connection, and get quotes early. With grid congestion in much of the country, check that the existing connection covers your peak load; see grid congestion.

Own warehouse or 3PL?

As a comparison: one Dutch storage provider lists about €7.70 per euro pallet per month for small volumes, so 2,000 pallets would cost about €185,000 a year for storage alone, before handling, picking and transport charges. An own warehouse becomes the better choice when volumes are steady, when you need service, repairs or a showroom, or when you want your own team. Many companies run both for a period; see finding a warehouse from abroad.

Frequently asked questions

What does a 3,000 m² warehouse in the Netherlands cost per year?

In the more affordable regions, rent and service charges come to roughly €225,000 a year (€65 rent plus about €10 service charges per m²). Near Rotterdam or Schiphol it is considerably more. Staff, equipment, energy and insurance come on top.

How much should we budget for the first year of a European warehouse?

For a 3,000 m² warehouse with racking, two reach trucks, six operatives and a small showroom, budget roughly €720,000–990,000 in year one, excluding VAT, energy, insurance and IT. About €210,000–450,000 of that is one-off.

How big is the guarantee for a Dutch warehouse lease?

Usually about three months' rent plus service charges and VAT, as a bank guarantee or deposit. A company from abroad without Dutch accounts may be asked for more, or for a parent-company guarantee.

What does pallet racking cost?

Roughly €80–150 per pallet location installed for standard pallet racking. The price depends on height, load and safety features, and high racking has to fit the building's fire-safety rules.

What does a warehouse worker cost in the Netherlands?

Operatives typically earn about €15–18 an hour gross in 2026. With 8% holiday allowance, pension and employer charges, we plan with about €43,000–47,000 a year per full-time operative; check it with a payroll provider.
